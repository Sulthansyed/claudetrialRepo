# Merdeka, 1946–1957 (30 s): treatment

Style: Comic History Cartoon · 1080×1920 (9:16) · 24 fps · 30 s · English narrator (Kokoro bm_george), burned-in captions (`subtitles_en.srt`).
This is the long cut of the 10-second reel in `../merdeka-1957/`. It uses the same puppets, map and peel, and adds the first ten years of the story.

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **One piece of land, three paper actions.** In 1946 the patch is slapped on, in 1956 the papers are stamped, and in 1957 the patch is peeled off. **Chosen**: each panel is a different native move, and the story's turn (from imposed, to negotiated, to free) is carried by the paper itself.
2. **A civilian's life in corner panels** (a Malay schoolboy in 1946 grows up to vote in 1955 and watch the 1957 ceremony). *Rejected*: it needs a third puppet sheet and more reading time than 30 s allows.
3. **Layer on layer**: Portuguese, Dutch and British patches pasted over Malacca, all pulled off in 1957. *Rejected*: it suggests 1957 undid four centuries, which isn't true, and it crowds the frame.

## 2. Logline and arc
Britain slaps one big patch over the Malay states. It never lies flat, and eleven years later Malaya peels it off with Britain's agreement.
- Setup (1946): the Malayan Union is imposed. NO UNION! The corner curls up.
- Turn (1956): in London both sides sign; AGREED; a handshake.
- Ending (1957): the peel, MERDEKA!, a pin on Kuala Lumpur, then a pull back to the whole page and the title card.

## 3. Benchmark
- Learn from **Saul Bass cut-paper title work** (one gesture per shot, hard paper edges) and **vertical history explainer shorts** (a hook in the first second, a caption band you can read with the sound off, one idea per beat).
- Don't take: their characters, layouts, typefaces, music or specific shots.

## 5. Beat sheet
- 0.2 year tag **1946**, "The Malayan Union". 0.6–2.1: Britain hops in, holding the giant patch up by its east edge (big shadow). 2.6 wind-up (tremble, tremolo). 3.0 one in-between frame with ink arcs. **3.25 SLAP**, on "patch". Malaya's songkok jumps.
- 7.45 **NO UNION!** with a stomp and two puffs of steam. 9.5 the Johor corner curls up and flaps ("won't lie flat"); Britain is shocked, then strains.
- 11.1 tag **1956 London**. The agreement reads "Independence / for the Federation of Malaya / by August 1957". **14.3 AGREED** stamp. 15.2 handshake.
- 17.45 tag **1957**, "Federation of Malaya". 18.6–19.6 Malaya hops in. 20.1 grip. 20.6 / 21.3 / 22.0 three tugs. 22.3 Britain sighs. 23.15 "lets go". **23.45 the peel**: the patch is flung out of frame and the helmet jumps. 23.6 slip "31 August 1957". 23.7 **MERDEKA!** 23.95 pin on **Kuala Lumpur**. Malaya hops for joy.
- 26.4 pull back. **27.2 title card** "merdeka · Malaya, 1946–1957". Hold to 30.

## 6. Cue map
Britain is tuba and trombone oom-pah; Malaya is hand drums (darbuka doom and tak, standing in for gendang and rebana) and gong.
- **0–3.25:** march with xylophone hops, tremolo wind-up, band hit on the slap.
- **3.75–9.5:** smug oom-pah. Drums answer NO UNION! (7.45). The oom-pah falters at the curl (9.5).
- **10.9–16.5:** a woodblock clock ticks while brass and drums take turns. Timpani on the stamp. Both accents together on the handshake. A drum fill leads into the drop.
- **17.5–23.45:** thinning oom-pah. Drums on the hops and on each tug. Violin tremolo from 22.3.
- **23.45 to the end:**
  - 23.45: big gong, bass drum and marimba run.
  - 23.95: the pin stops everything dead, leaving a small gong ringing.
  - 24.45–26.4: a drum and marimba celebration.
  - 27.36: the full ensemble on Bb with the title card, ringing out.

Voice is about 10 dB over the music. While it speaks, music and foley duck 3 dB, and the music's 300 Hz–4 kHz band a further 6.5 dB. Between lines they come back up. Master −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dB.

## 9. Not the demo again
This film's structure (three panels, one per paper action) differs from the demo's six panels and four changes of hand. So do its opening (a patch carried in over a map that is already coloured, with the owner watching), its signature move (a peel walked back by tugs), its 9:16 page laid out in rows, and its score shape (two accents trading places, then playing together). It shares the stamp, the pin and the title card (3 of 6).

# OCLC · KAFA Satu Klik — Treatment

Style: **Paper Pop-up Book** (`paper-popup`). 30 s, vertical 1080×1920 (9:16), for TikTok / Reels.
Client: OCLC (One Click Learning Centre), online KAFA classes for kids. Audience: busy Malaysian Muslim parents.
Language: Malay only (voice and captions). **No Arabic, Jawi or Quran text anywhere**: every book on screen is a plain cover or a blank page.

## As built (after the real voice was recorded)

The Malay narration (ms-MY-YasminNeural) ran about 1.5 s longer than the estimates below, so the film was re-timed; `story.js` holds the final numbers:
- Length **30.5 s**, 1080×1920, **30 fps**, −14 LUFS.
- Grid **104.35 bpm in 4/4** (beat 0.575 s, bar 2.3 s), origin at 10.0 s; Klik's click at 3.675 s is on a beat and starts the music.
- Climax pages land on bar lines **10.0 / 12.3 / 14.6 / 16.9 s**; home again at 19.2 s; end card from 24.25 s (sign drops 24.375 s, held 6 s).
- Line 4 is four separate phrases, one per page; line 5 is four sentences from 18.7 s, ending 30.05 s.
- Score as built: ukulele strums, glockenspiel, contrabass and violin pizzicato, shaker, woodblock, vibraphone final chord.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One spread pulled again and again.** One kitchen spread stays open the whole film; each pull-tab changes it (clock spins, car slides in and folds, laptop slides up, stars fill). Clear but static, and the user's brief asks for four page flips, which this fights.
2. **Two books facing each other.** A "before" book (stress: clock, traffic) and an "after" book (OCLC) on the desk, Klik hopping across the gap. Strong comparison, but at 9:16 two books are tiny, and 30 s doesn't leave time to establish both.
3. **One book, turned page by page (chosen).** The book opens on a stressed mum, Klik pops out of the page, a page turn takes us into the traffic jam, Klik's click folds it flat, then four quick page turns are the four steps, and the last turn brings us back to the same home spread, now calm. **Why:** page turn = scene change is the medium's own grammar, it maps 1:1 onto the brief's beats, and the bookend (same home spread, stress → calm, same clock now still) gives the ending an echo.

Opening: the closed book is already opening at frame 1 (no slow reveal). Ending: **one piece stays up**, the family spread folds down and Klik stays standing to hold the end-card sign.
Score shape: no music in the hook (room tone and a clock ticking too fast); the music starts **on Klik's click**; it builds through the four pages and resolves on the end card.

## 2. Logline and arc

A stressed mum finds that KAFA for her child doesn't need the school run: one click, and the class comes home.

- **Setup**: book opens, mum pops up and stares at a racing clock.
- **Turn**: Klik pops out of the page with a "klik!"; the traffic jam the family would sit in folds flat and away.
- **Ending**: four pages = four steps (WhatsApp → small group on a laptop → teacher listens ✓ → stars fill), then home again, smiling; Klik holds up the OCLC sign with the WhatsApp number.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Robert Sabuda / Matthew Reinhart pop-up books.** *Learn:* pieces rising back to front, V-folds, pull-tabs, one dominant colour per spread. *Don't take:* any specific spread, character or paper engineering design.
- **Short "problem → one tap → solved" app spots on TikTok.** *Learn:* the hook in the first second, one idea per beat, the call to action held long enough to read. *Don't take:* their shots, music, UI or logos.

## 4. Characters (all flat cut paper, off-white border, grey card edge)

- **Klik**: the OCLC mascot. A teal/navy cube drawn in 3/4 view on one flat card (top face light teal, front face teal, side face navy), the front face is a dark screen with a simple face (two oval eyes, a smile, blinks). An antenna on top ends in a white mouse **cursor arrow**. Floats with a slow bob; its "click" is the cursor tip pressing down.
- **Mum**: a young Malay mother in a soft rose tudung and long-sleeved baju kurung (mustard). Modest, friendly face; expressions: stressed (brows up and in, small mouth), relieved, smiling.
- **Child**: a boy about 8 in a navy songkok and a sky-blue baju Melayu. Expressions: bored (in the car), focused (reciting), proud.
- **Ustazah** (the teacher, step ③): a teacher in a lilac tudung, hand to her ear, listening, kind smile.
- **Small group** (step ②): four tiny kids on the laptop screen in tiles (two boys in songkok, two girls in tudung) plus a teacher tile.

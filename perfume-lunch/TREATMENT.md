# Why your perfume disappears by lunch — treatment (25 s, 1080x1920)

Logline: a scent cloud is a living thing with three layers; heat strips the top, dry skin lets go of the rest; three small habits keep it till evening.

## Three candidate structures
A. **Clock-spine** — one big clock face stays on screen the whole film; every beat is the clock moving (8→12, 12→12, 12→6). Clear but busy; clock competes with the bottle.
B. **Cloud-as-character** — the scent cloud is the hero: 100% → splits → burned → rebuilt. The clock is a small chip top-right that only appears in hook and payoff. Bottle anchors the lower third; cloud fills the middle.
C. **Before/after split** — screen halves, "your routine" vs "fixed routine". Strong for comparison, weak for the 3-layer reveal.

**Choice: B.** Cloud is the subject, so the 3-layer split, the sun burning the top layer, and the final "stays strong" are all the same object changing. Clock chip + % readout carry time.

## Beats
1. 0–3 hook: bottle low-centre, cloud 100%, clock chip 8AM→12PM spins, cloud fades to 0%. Neon text "RM300 PERFUME… GONE BY LUNCH?"
2. 3–8 curiosity: cloud returns, splits into 3 stacked bands TOP / HEART / BASE with labels.
3. 8–14 conflict: sun "32°C" top-right; top band burns off fast; below, a dry-skin texture strip and the base/heart slide sideways off it.
4. 14–21 climax: three fix cards pop in one by one, icons: ① moisturiser jar ② pulse dot + shirt ③ wrist with a crossed-out rub arrows.
5. 21–25 payoff: clock runs to 6PM, cloud stays strong (≥80%), neon end card "Comment your perfume 👇".

# Misty Ridge — treatment

**Style:** Watercolor Brush · **Length:** 30 s · **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps (the style's slow brush wants more than 24) · **Language:** English, narrator + burned-in subtitles
**Brief (defaults):** an invented family coffee farm, "Misty Ridge Farm", Arabica on a misty hillside at 1,450 m, planted by the narrator's grandfather in 1987. No user material: everything painted, original score.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Cherry to cup, one tall sheet.** Steps painted top to bottom (seedling → tree → cherry → drying → roast → cup), the camera tilting down. Clear, but a recipe; it reads as a process diagram, not a home.
2. **A year on one locked page.** One coffee tree, painted over and over through the seasons: rain, blossom, green cherries, red cherries, harvest. Beautiful, but static for 30 s and no place, no people.
3. **The drop and the cup (chosen).** A single drop of coffee falls on blank paper and blooms into the hillside at dawn: the coffee is the pigment. We walk the slope, lean into one branch as it blossoms and ripens, watch the harvest fill a basket, and end on a cup whose dark surface holds the hill again. The film paints its subject with its subject, and the ending bookends the opening drop.

Why 3: it has a hook in the first two seconds (a drop on white paper), a native move only watercolour can do (wet pigment spreading into a landscape), a turn (green to red, blossom to harvest), and an echo ending (drop → hill → cup → hill).

## 2. Logline and arc

*One drop of coffee spreads into the hill it came from.*

- **Setup** (0–9 s): the drop blooms into Misty Ridge at dawn; the slope and its rows of trees paint themselves in.
- **Turn** (9–21 s): one branch blossoms white, then its cherries swell and blush red; hands-free harvest: ripe cherries drop into a woven basket, then dry on a raised bed.
- **Ending** (21–30 s): the brush lifts (near-silence), mist washes the page to paper, a cup paints in, and its coffee surface shows the hill. Note: *Misty Ridge Farm · since 1987*. The last stroke stops mid-way.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Naturalist field-journal plates** (specimen + hand note) and **slow nature-documentary openings** that move from a macro detail to a landscape.
- *Learn:* the macro-to-wide scale reveal; one hero subject per view with a quiet note; unhurried pacing; restraint in colour.
- *Don't take:* any specific artwork, plate layout, shot or melody.

## 6. Cue map

- **Tempo** 80 bpm, 4/4, 10 bars. Key D major (warm, open strings).
- Bar 1: no music (paper, drip). Bars 2–3: kalimba arpeggio + soft pad. Bars 4–5: kalimba alone, sparse (the breath), glockenspiel on the blossom burst (4.2). Bars 6–7: fingerpicked guitar enters, kalimba answers; cherry drops on beats 6.2, 6.3, 6.4, 7.1. Bar 8: out (silence 21.0–22.5), pad returns on 8.3. Bars 9–10: guitar + kalimba, softer; final D chord on 10.3, ringing out.

## 9. Subtitles and titles

- Subtitles on the paper, centred at the bottom, Cormorant Garamond italic ~36 px, ink at ~78 % over a paper-coloured halo; one language only, so a single line in the upper line's place.
- Hand notes in Caveat with pen leader lines: *Arabica · 1,450 m* (shot 2), *Misty Ridge Farm · since 1987* (shot 6).
- No end card or logo: the end note is the sign-off.

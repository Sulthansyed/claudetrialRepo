# RM750 or RM0? — treatment

**Style:** Isometric Infographic · **Channel:** Ringgit, Explained (TikTok) · **Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps · **Length:** 22.8 s (voice-led timing, approved by the client)
**Voice:** Kokoro `am_onyx`, speed 0.92, English · **Subtitles:** BM line large on top, English line small under it · **Mix:** for phone speakers

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One desk, two timelines.** A single worker; the film rewinds and replays the year with and without PRS. Clean, but "two people" is the user's hook and a rewind is harder to read on a phone.
2. **A route through the tax system.** Follow one salary as a coin from payslip → LHDN → refund. Good process film, but it explains tax in general, not the decision.
3. **Two boards stacked (chosen).** The vertical frame holds two identical isometric desks, one above the other: Aisyah (top) and Ali (bottom). Between them, off to the side, a third small plate: a locked PRS vault. Each board has its own counter. The film switches focus between the boards and ends with both side by side again, numbers different.

Why 3: 9:16 is made for two stacked dioramas; "same salary" is shown by two identical boards rather than said; the only difference on screen is one route line into the vault, which is the whole argument.

## 2. Logline and arc

*Two identical desks, one locked box: the only difference between RM750 and RM0 is a route line.*

- **Hook** (0–3 s): both boards, counters pop: RM750 (top) vs RM0 (bottom).
- **Curiosity** (3–7 s): counters rewind to RM0; the camera slides to the PRS vault, padlocked, a "?" ring around it.
- **Conflict** (7–12 s): Ali's board in focus, the rest greys. His salary stack flows down a route line into the TAX block. His counter stays RM0 (a small shake).
- **Climax** (12–18 s): Aisyah's board in focus. Three RM1,000 bricks slide into the PRS vault, the lid opens (cutaway), a TAX RELIEF stamp lands, coins come back along the route and her counter climbs to RM750 (15 Isotype coins of RM50). Footnote under the board.
- **Payoff** (18–22 s): pull out to both boards; a calendar block flips to 31 DIS; "DEADLINE: 31 DEC" in isometric type; end card "Nak versi BM penuh?" with a comment bubble.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Kurzgesagt-style explainers** and **isometric annual-report infographics**: learn the one-metaphor-per-step pacing and the "numbers built from icons" grammar.
- **Don't take:** characters, icons, palettes, logos or music. No bank, fund-provider or LHDN logo appears; the tax office is a generic block labelled TAX.

## 6. Cue map

- 96 bpm, light and bright: marimba motif for Aisyah (rises), a dull woodblock for Ali, pizzicato bass, shaker.
- Hook: marimba hit on the RM750 pop, woodblock on RM0. Curiosity: music drops to bass + shaker, a short near-silence before the padlock rattle. Conflict: woodblock on each coin into TAX, minor colour. Climax: full groove; one rising marimba note per returning coin; stamp on the downbeat. Payoff: motif resolved, last hit on the end card.

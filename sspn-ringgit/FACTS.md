## Arithmetic and staging that are not claims

- **What-if in beat 3:** 8,000 deposited − 3,000 withdrawn = 5,000 relief; 5,000 × 25 % = RM1,250. Shown as a what-if and undone with ctrl-Z; 3,000 is an illustrative number.
- **"1 sel = RM80":** RM8,000 × 1 % = RM80, so the bars are 11, 19 and 25 cells tall. A display unit, not a claim.
- **Aisyah and Ali** are fictional; both are put in the 25 % band so that only the account differs. "Saved" is the tax relief × band, i.e. the most the relief can save at that band ("up to", "depending on your tax band"); the footnote says "Jimat bergantung pada kadar cukai · Bukan nasihat kewangan / Not financial advice" and "Untuk YA 2026".

## Not claimed

- Nothing about whether both parents can each claim RM8,000 for the same child (the sources disagree). The film shows two separate parents with separate accounts and never says they are a couple or share a child.
